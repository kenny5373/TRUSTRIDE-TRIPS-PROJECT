# TRUSTRIDE-TRIPS-PROJECT
The first quarter of the year the company has been having trips scheduled but the revenue doesn't show to it, they are trying to figure out why they are having this problem

**Business Problems**
The company noticed that the first quarter of the year they had members who called to schedule appointment trips with them but looking at the revenue made they noticed deficit from the cost. they are also trying to figure out why the members where cancelling their appointment trips.

**Objectives**
1. Analyze trips booked and completed
2. Analyze time performance
3. Analysze most distance covered

2. Pivot Tables'

4. dashboard designs
